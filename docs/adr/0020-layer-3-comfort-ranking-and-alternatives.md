# 0020: Layer 3 Phase 3A: comfort ratio, plan ranking and smarter alternatives

Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-11-14). Covers Layer 3 steps 3.0, 3.2 and 3.3 (Phase 3A, ADR 0012).

## Context
Phase 3A builds the comfort engine, plan ranking and smarter alternatives as pure domain code, plus the customer API contract, before Layer 2 is merged. The design (C4, C5, C6 and `Main`) gives sample numbers for all three. One of them cannot be reproduced by the EMI engine, and several rules the screens imply (what "monthly amount" means for a plan whose last installment differs, how a ratio is rounded, how plans are ordered) are not written down anywhere.

## Decision
1. **Down-payment alternative: the engine is the source of truth.** C6 and `Main` say "₹4,000 down gives ₹2,999 a month, 36.6%". The engine gives ₹22,000 − ₹4,000 = ₹18,000 no-cost over 6 months = ₹3,000.00 a month, and (₹8,000 + ₹3,000) / ₹30,000 = 36.67%, which is 3,667 basis points (36.7%). Even ₹2,999 would give 36.66%, which rounds half-up to 36.7%. The owner chose the engine figures; the mockup numbers are an error. On the design data the down-payment and the cheaper-model (64 GB at ₹18,000) alternatives therefore both show ₹3,000 a month and 36.7%.
2. **The down payment is derived, not configured.** D = (the chosen plan's total payable − monthly budget × chosen tenure), rounded **up** to the next ₹1,000 step, so a plan with an extra cost is judged on everything it collects. The step is held in one configuration value. There is no down-payment alternative when that gap is zero or below (the plan already fits) or when D would be the whole price or more. The chosen plan is re-quoted on price − D under the same offer id and shown honestly, even if it is still above the budget. On the design data (no-cost plan A, total ₹22,000) D is ₹4,000; with a ₹1,800 budget it is ₹12,000 (gap ₹11,200); plan B (total ₹23,100) with a ₹3,700 budget gives a gap of ₹900, so D is ₹1,000 and the re-quote on ₹21,000 is ₹3,675 a month.
3. **Monthly commitment** used for the budget fit and the comfort ratio is the largest single installment of the plan: the maximum of the lender's quoted monthly amount, our schedule's regular installment and its last installment. This is conservative, and on the design data it equals the monthly amount shown (plan A: ₹3,666.67).
4. **Comfort ratio** = (existing EMIs + plan monthly commitment) / monthly inflow, kept exact. On the wire it is an integer in basis points, rounded half-up. The band is classified on those **rounded** basis points, so the percentage shown and the band can never disagree. Bands are demo heuristics, **[Verify]** with a lender or risk team before any pilot: below 3,500 bp Comfortable; 3,500 to 5,000 bp inclusive Stretching; above 5,000 bp Risky. Exactly 35.00% and exactly 50.00% are Stretching. Design check: A 3,889 bp, B 3,950 bp, C 3,350 bp; the Risky example (inflow ₹20,000, plan B) is 5,925 bp.
5. **Ranking order** (from C4): plans within the budget first, then by the amount above the budget ascending; ties by extra in total ascending, then tenure ascending, then offer id. No plan is ever dropped. When no plan fits, every plan is returned labelled above budget, with the amount above. Reason codes state facts only: `WITHIN_BUDGET`, `ABOVE_BUDGET`, `NO_EXTRA_COST`, `LOWEST_MONTHLY_AMOUNT`, `LOWEST_EXTRA_IN_TOTAL`. There is no "recommended" or "best" code (CLAUDE.md section 2, rule 7).
6. **Synthetic financial profiles** sit behind `FinancialProfilePort` with a labelled Mock: the standard profile has an inflow of ₹30,000 and existing EMIs of ₹8,000; the risky profile has an inflow of ₹20,000 and existing EMIs of ₹8,000, which gives the design's Risky 59.3% for plan B ((₹8,000 + ₹3,850) / ₹20,000 = 5,925 bp).
7. **The Phase 3A contract is exported without being mounted.** A separate contract application builds only the customer routers and writes `backend/openapi.customer.json`. The routers are mounted in `main.py` in Phase 3C (ADR 0012).

## Consequences
Every number on C4, C5 and C6 comes from the EMI engine and the comfort engine, never from the mockups. The C6 mockup text (₹2,999, 36.6%) must not be copied into fixtures or the frontend. A plan whose last installment is larger than its regular one is judged on the larger amount. Thresholds live in one configuration class and are labelled as heuristics.
