# 0018: Merchant reports, settlement Mock and the template weekly message

Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-11-14/15: Layer 2 plan Q3, Q9, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q21; Q12 refined during step 2.4 to keep the design's numbers).

## Decision
- **Funnel:** an append-only `funnel_event` table (STARTED, BUDGET_TOLD, PLANS_VIEWED, AGREED, PAID), written in the same transaction as the sale change. Reports are cohorts: sales **started** in the period, counted by the stages they reached. Step progress never sits on the sale row the merchant reads.
- **Periods:** TODAY, THIS_WEEK (Monday to Sunday) and THIS_MONTH in `Asia/Kolkata`, from the injected Clock.
- **Open sales are not reported (gate review B2, rule 6):** every report cohort leaves out sales still CHOOSING. A sale enters the reports once it is READY_TO_PAY, PAID, PAYMENT_NOT_COMPLETED or EXPIRED, states the merchant already sees on the counter list; otherwise reloading "Today" would follow one customer's steps and budget band live.
- **Small counts (owner, Q3):** every count from 1 to 4 that leaves the API is suppressed (`customerCount: null, isSuppressed: true`), including suggestion parameters; budget buckets are hidden when fewer than 5 customers told a budget. A zero right after a suppressed funnel stage is suppressed too (cascading). The biggest drop is omitted (null) when either neighbouring stage is suppressed, because naming the stage would show where those few customers stopped; a drop of 1 to 4 between two shown stages is suppressed but named. When exactly one non-zero budget bucket is suppressed, the smallest other non-zero bucket is suppressed too (complementary suppression), so it cannot be recovered as the total minus the shown buckets. The budget total is reported exactly as the funnel reports BUDGET_TOLD (a zero after a suppressed cohort is suppressed; a zero after an empty or shown cohort is shown), and a zero missed count follows the budget total, so no figure contradicts or out-reveals the funnel. A suppressed figure is sent as `null`; every suppressed budget bucket reads "Hidden" on screen, because a companion bucket may hold 5 or more. When the missed count is suppressed, the ceiling, the product insight, the under-ceiling flags and the missed-sales suggestions are withheld. One `SmallCountSuppressionPolicy` holds the threshold.
- **Budget buckets:** ₹2,500, ₹3,000, ₹3,500, ₹4,000 boundaries, lower bound inclusive.
- **Missed sale:** reached BUDGET_TOLD, never AGREED, and the budget is below the cheapest no-extra-cost monthly amount of its product. The headline ceiling is the smallest bucket boundary at or above every missed budget (₹3,000 with the seed). The approved plan wording would have chosen ₹3,500 and broken the design's "12 under ₹3,000", so the rule was refined.
- **Suggested actions (Pattern: Strategy):** `LongerNoCostPlanSuggestion` and `CheaperModelSuggestion` return reason codes and paise parameters; amounts come from the existing EMI strategies.
- **Money due:** `SettlementPort` with `MockSettlementAdapter` returning the three design batches for RAVI-0193 (₹72,000 on 13 Jan, ₹64,000 on 28 Jan, ₹48,000 on 12 Feb 2027). Not filtered by the range. Announced at startup (ADR 0006).
- **Weekly message:** built from a template in English and draft Tamil by the backend, because it is a sent text artefact like Layer 4's AI text. It uses one formatter, `core/indian_rupee_formatter.py`, an explicit exception to backend.md §5 ("format for display only in the frontend"). Wording follows the numbers (Q9): "The biggest drop was after seeing the plans", and the suggestion quotes the computed amounts. "Send to my phone" goes through `MockWeeklyMessageDeliveryAdapter`, which sends nothing and only records `sent_at` (owner decision). Layer 4 replaces the template text behind the same endpoint.
- **Seed:** `make seed` writes 121 sales relative to the Clock so THIS_WEEK equals DESIGN_SPEC §4 exactly (120/96/88/51/44, drop 37, buckets 5/7/14/21/49, 12 missed, ₹1,84,000), plus the three M2 counter sales today (4821, 4817, 4812). 4821 is CHOOSING and therefore outside the reports until it expires, so one extra finished sale keeps the totals. It runs only when the shop has no sales; `make db-reset seed` reseeds for a new week.

## Consequences
- About an hour after seeding, 4821 expires and joins the reports (STARTED 121, "44 of 121"); reseed before a demo.
- A CHOOSING sale past its deadline enters the reports only once its expiry is recorded (on the next counter read or sale command); reports do not run the expiry sweep.
- **Residual risk (owner decision pending):** when one sale leaves CHOOSING, reloading M5 moves the stage counts and one budget bucket by one, right after the counter list showed which sale changed. Suppression protects only small totals. A stronger rule would report closed days only.
- A drop of 1 to 4 between two shown stages can be recovered by subtracting them; accepted under Q3's per-cell rule.
- Report endpoints that call the gateway (missed sales, suggested actions, weekly message) have no rate limit yet, and one M5 load quotes every catalogue product about three times; share or cache quotes before paytm mode.
- Money-due batch sale counts are not suppressed (they describe the shop's own settlements, not customers).
