Rules every screen follows

  • Only three comfort bands: Comfortable, Stretching, Risky. Each has an icon and a word, never colour alone.
  • Every plan shows “Each month ₹X for N months” beside “Extra in total ₹Y on top of the price”, at the same size, so nobody reads the extra as monthly.
  • No interest rates, no card fields, no “best for you”, no urgency.
  • The merchant never sees inflow, EMIs, comfort ratios or the customer’s step-by-step progress; only “Choosing” or “Ready to pay”, the chosen plan and totals.
  • Merchant app has two tabs in shop language: New EMI sale and Sales report. Everything else opens in place on the report as an expandable card, never as a separate page.
  • Every number the AI says is also on screen. Voice is optional.
  • One main action per customer screen. Language toggle on every customer screen.
  • Tamil strings are drafts: a native speaker must review them before the demo.
